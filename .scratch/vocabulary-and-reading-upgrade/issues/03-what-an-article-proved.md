# 03: What an Article proved

**What to build:** An **Article** remembers its **Banked Result** — the **Words** it made **Known**, the **Clean Sightings** it advanced, the **Lookups** made in it — and shows it on its row. Today that answer exists for one screen after 读完 and is then gone, so an Article finished in March is indistinguishable from one never opened.

This is what gives a finished Article, and an **Archived Article**, a reason to be on the shelf.

**Blocked by:** 02 (the per-Article Clean Sighting records are what this reads; without them it would be a second count to keep in step)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## The rule

- [ ] Finishing an Article for the first time stores what it proved, alongside the fact that it was banked
- [ ] Rereading changes nothing, including this: the stored result is from the reading that earned it
- [ ] The result survives archiving and restoring
- [ ] It is a record of that moment, not a live query: a Word looked up next week does not rewrite what an Article proved in March

## The screen

- [ ] An Article's row says what it gave, in a quiet line under its existing meta
- [ ] An Article never finished says nothing there rather than showing zeros
- [ ] Archived Articles show theirs too — it is most of the reason to look at the archived list
- [ ] The student's own titles and **Source** are never translated

## Tests

- [ ] An Article banked with two Words reaching three sightings records two made Known
- [ ] An Article whose Words were all looked up records that, and is not recorded as empty
- [ ] A second 读完 leaves the stored result untouched
- [ ] Archiving and restoring leaves it untouched
- [ ] A Word looked up in a *later* Article does not change what an earlier one recorded

## Glossary

- [ ] **Banked Result** goes into `CONTEXT.md` once it exists

## Left for the iPhone

- [ ] Finish an Article and check the line reads like something worth having, not like a receipt
- [ ] Open the archived list and see whether the results make it worth keeping
