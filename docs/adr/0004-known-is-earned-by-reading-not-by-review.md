# Known is earned by reading, not by review

A **Word** becomes **Known** when the student has read it in three different **Articles** without once tapping it for help. There is no spaced repetition, no review queue and nothing due: the evidence is a by-product of reading, which the student was going to do anyway.

The obvious design is a flashcard deck with widening intervals. It was rejected because it is a second daily obligation beside the **Daily Checklist** the student already keeps, and an abandoned deck is the ordinary ending of a self-study vocabulary app. The **Goal** is to talk to people in Hangzhou, not to clear a queue. Reading real Chinese the student chose is the behaviour worth rewarding, so that is the behaviour the number is built out of: importing more Articles is the only way to move it.

## Considered Options

- **Spaced repetition over every unmet Word.** The most accurate measure of recall, and the one most likely to be silently abandoned in week three. Rejected on sustainability, not on accuracy.
- **The student marks Words Known by hand, and only that.** No bookkeeping at all, but the headline number then measures optimism. Kept as an override, not as the rule.
- **Count every appearance of a Word, not every Article.** Cheaper to compute, but a Word repeated nine times in one text would be Known from a single reading, which is not evidence of anything. Hence one **Clean Sighting** per Article, first reading only.

## Consequences

- A **Lookup** is destructive on purpose: it returns the Word to zero and all three Articles must be earned again. This is what makes an untapped Word mean something.
- Rereading an Article banks nothing. Evidence is counted when an Article is finished for the first time, so `Article` has to remember that it has been banked, not merely that it has been read.
- The number is generous where the student reads narrowly: a word met in three articles on tea is Known on tea alone. Accepted. The cost of being wrong is one word, and 认识 / 不认识 in **Daily New Words** is the correction.
- Nothing in the app is ever due, so nothing can be late. No badge, no streak, no debt for a day not opened. A reminder to read belongs on the **Daily Checklist** as an ordinary **Routine** the student wrote themselves.
